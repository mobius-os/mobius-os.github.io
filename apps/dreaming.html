Dreaming

While you sleep, Möbius dreams: it interviews the agents that worked during the day, sharpens its own skills and memory, hardens your apps, and leaves you a one-page morning brief with a feedback path into chat. Quiet nights are skipped — your streak resets when you take a night off.

Reads offline Runs daily Reads other apps (read)
v2.0.5 by mobius-os MIT Source ↗ ★ 0 updated 2026-06-06

Install

Open the App Store mini-app in your Möbius shell and paste: 

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mobius-os/app-dreaming/main/mobius.json

Möbius shows the requested permissions and the cron schedule, then installs.
