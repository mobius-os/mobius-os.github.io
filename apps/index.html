Curated apps

A small, hand-picked catalog. Anyone can install any of these via the App Store mini-app inside Möbius, or by pasting the manifest URL.

News

v1.5.2

Daily AI-curated news digest. Customize what to search for and the delivery schedule from inside the app.

Works offline Runs daily at 10:00 UTC

Countries Been

v1.0.0

Tap a 3D globe to mark the countries you've visited. See them glow on the map, in a list, and as a count.

Works offline

Gym

v1.0.0

Lightweight training-program tracker. Pick a program (PPL, full-body, upper/lower, or custom) and log sessions set-by-set with a sticky rest timer, PR table, and a year-at-a-glance heatmap.

Works offline

LaTeX

v1.0.0

Math-first LaTeX editor. Describe what you want in prose; an AI sub-agent writes and edits the .tex files for you while you preview the math live.

Works offline

Dreaming

v1.1.0

A nightly agent reads what happened across your Möbius yesterday and writes a one-page morning report. Streak counter resets when you take a night off.

Works offline Runs daily at 06:00 UTC Reads other apps (read)

Submitting an app

To get listed here, see the contributing guide. The bar: a public repo with a valid mobius.json, a single-file index.jsx, and ideally a screenshot.
