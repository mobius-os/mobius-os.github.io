Log workouts by chatting, and it tracks your PRs, trends, and streaks.

Install

Open the App Store mini-app in your Möbius shell and paste: 

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mobius-os/app-workout/main/mobius.json

Möbius shows the requested permissions, then installs.
