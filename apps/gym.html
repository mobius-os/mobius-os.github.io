Gym

Lightweight training-program tracker. Pick a program (PPL, full-body, upper/lower, or custom) and log sessions set-by-set with a sticky rest timer, PR table, and a year-at-a-glance heatmap.

Works offline
v1.0.0 by mobius-os MIT Source ↗ ★ 0 updated 2026-05-30

Install

Open the App Store mini-app in your Möbius shell and paste: 

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mobius-os/app-gym/main/mobius.json

Möbius shows the requested permissions, then installs.
