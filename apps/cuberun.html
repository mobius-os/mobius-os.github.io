CubeRun

Neon 3D runner game packaged for Mobius as a durable static app.

v1.0.2-mobius.18 by Adam Karlsten, Mobius packaging by Hamza Merzic MIT Source ↗ ★ 0 updated 2026-07-13

Install

Open the App Store mini-app in your Möbius shell and paste: 

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mobius-os/app-cuberun/main/mobius.json

Möbius shows the requested permissions, then installs.
