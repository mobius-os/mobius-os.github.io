LaTeX

Write and compile LaTeX with live PDF preview and an agent that drafts your .tex.

Edits offline
v2.14.5 by mobius-os MIT Source ↗ ★ 0 updated 2026-07-13

Install

Open the App Store mini-app in your Möbius shell and paste: 

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mobius-os/app-latex/main/mobius.json

Möbius shows the requested permissions and the cron schedule, then installs.
